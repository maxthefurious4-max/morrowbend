Useful constraint

A strong-fit read does not mean “automate everything.” It means one small, human-reviewed lane may be worth testing.

Good first workflows are boring.

Look for repetitive intake, missing-information follow-up, status synthesis, document chasing, routing, and first-draft preparation. The goal is not autonomy. It is a dependable reduction in manual steps without losing control.

Some work should stay human.

Morrowbend does not decide, recommend, rank, screen, or materially support regulated or high-impact outcomes—including hiring, legal, medical, financial, or safety decisions—even with human review. If an error cannot be safely caught before action, it is not a fit for this sprint.
