Plain answers

Before anyone books a call.

What problem does Morrowbend solve?

Repeated administrative work: copying information, chasing missing details, rebuilding briefs or updates, and repairing fragile handoffs.

Will this replace people on my team?

No. The sprint removes avoidable repetition from one process. Your team remains responsible for judgment and approves external actions.

What tools do you use?

The smallest practical set for the workflow, favoring tools you already use. The service is not tied to one model, platform, or automation vendor.

Do you need sensitive data?

Not for an initial fit check. Do not send credentials, confidential records, regulated data, or sensitive personal information by email.

Why show a synthetic example?

There are no fabricated customer claims. The example shows the intended control flow using invented data; it is not production proof or a model evaluation.
