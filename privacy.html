Plain-language notice

Privacy without theater.

This launch site has no account, website form, advertising tracker, or upload field. Email contact is optional and is explained below.

Effective

September 4, 2026

Website interactions

The workflow fit check runs entirely in your browser. Its answers and result are not sent to Morrowbend, written to browser storage, or stored by the page. The site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral profiling.

Hosting records

The hosting provider may process basic technical records—such as IP address, requested page, browser type, and time of access—to deliver and secure the site. Those records are controlled by the hosting provider under its own terms.

Locally hosted fonts

The site serves its typefaces from the same host. It does not contact Google Fonts or another font service when a page loads.

Email inquiries

If you choose to email Morrowbend, the message is handled by Google and Morrowbend receives the address you send from, message content, standard email metadata, and any attachment you choose to include. This information is used only to respond, evaluate workflow fit, prevent abuse, and maintain necessary business or legal records. Do not send confidential records, credentials, regulated data, sensitive personal information, or client files.

Inquiries that do not become client relationships are deleted within 90 days after the final substantive exchange, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for security, legal, or recordkeeping obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing maxthefurious4@gmail.com with the subject “Morrowbend privacy request.”

LinkedIn conversations

If you copy a fit-check result and send it through LinkedIn, that message is governed by LinkedIn’s privacy practices and the choices you make in the conversation. Do not send passwords, access tokens, regulated data, or confidential client information.

Client work

Discovery is limited to synthetic or de-identified descriptions. No live records, documents, credentials, integrations, or system access are accepted until the counsel-approved client agreements and data schedule are signed and the required security, retention, deletion, and subprocessor controls are in place. This website notice is not a substitute for that agreement.

Contact and service providers

Contact Morrowbend through the contact page. The temporary inbox is operated through Google, and this website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms. Morrowbend does not sell inquiry data or use it for advertising.
