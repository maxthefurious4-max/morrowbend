Plain-language notice

Privacy without theater.

This launch site is deliberately simple: no account, no contact form, no advertising tracker, and no upload field.

Effective

September 2, 2026

What this site collects

The website itself does not ask you to submit personal information. The workflow fit check runs in your browser. Its answers and result are not sent to Morrowbend or stored by the page.

Hosting records

The hosting provider may process basic technical records—such as IP address, requested page, browser type, and time of access—to deliver and secure the site. Those records are controlled by the hosting provider under its own terms.

Locally hosted fonts

The site serves its typefaces from the same host. It does not contact Google Fonts or another font service when a page loads.

LinkedIn conversations

If you copy a fit-check result and send it through LinkedIn, that message is governed by LinkedIn’s privacy practices and the choices you make in the conversation. Do not send passwords, access tokens, regulated data, or confidential client information.

Client work

Any paid engagement will use a separate written scope covering access, data minimization, retention, subprocessors, security responsibilities, and approved use. This website notice is not a substitute for that agreement.

Contact

Until a dedicated business contact address is activated, contact Morrowbend through the LinkedIn conversation in which you received this website.
