What your team gets in 10 days.

Morrowbend takes one recurring admin workflow—such as client intake, follow-up, or status reporting—and builds a clearer working version that reduces repeated copying and catches missing details before a person approves the result.

The customer outcome

The goal is less manual copying, less repeated follow-up, fewer dropped details, and a documented process your team can run without depending on Morrowbend.

The sprint, day by day

Day 1–2 · Map

A 45-minute working session defines the trigger, inputs, exceptions, people, systems, approval point, and current baseline. If the workflow cannot fit the sprint, the build stops before it becomes a custom project.

Day 3–6 · Build

We connect the narrow path using your existing tools where possible. The workflow may extract information, structure a brief, identify gaps, prepare a draft, create a task, or update a tracker—but it does not bypass the agreed approval boundary.

Day 7–8 · Break it deliberately

We test missing inputs, conflicting instructions, duplicates, low-confidence output, sensitive information, unavailable integrations, and the cases that should escalate to a person.

Day 9–10 · Hand off

Your owner reviews the final workflow. You receive the map, logs, failure notes, operating instructions, a short training session, and the measurement plan.

The deliverable is not “AI strategy.” It is a small operating change your team can inspect, accept, or reject.

What is included

  • 45-minute workflow mapping session
  • One narrow automation or semi-automation
  • Human approval before external sends or irreversible updates
  • Missing-information and confidence flags where useful
  • Simple event log or trace
  • Failure-case and “do not automate” checklist
  • Handoff documentation and 30-minute training
  • Current baseline record and post-launch measurement plan
  • Seven-day bug-fix window after handoff

What is not included

  • A custom application or SaaS product
  • Multiple unrelated workflows
  • CRM migration or broad systems integration
  • Regulated legal, medical, financial, or hiring decisions
  • Autonomous external actions without agreed review
  • Open-ended strategy, change management, or unlimited support
  • Work that depends on confidential material from any employer

Founding beta terms

$1,500 for up to two qualified teams. Fifty percent at kickoff and fifty percent at handoff. Fast, candid feedback is requested. Any public use of outcomes, identity, quotes, or screenshots requires separate written approval and is never a condition of the beta price.

If mapping shows the workflow cannot be responsibly delivered in the signed scope, the engagement stops and the kickoff payment is refunded.

Good fit

  • 3–25 person B2B agency or professional-services team
  • A workflow repeated at least weekly
  • Email, forms, documents, spreadsheets, CRM, or project tools are involved
  • Slow response, manual copying, missing context, or dropped follow-up has a visible cost
  • One owner can review decisions within 24–48 hours
  • The team wants a practical process—not an AI trend presentation

Not a fit

  • The desired outcome requires broad or regulated judgment
  • No one owns the workflow
  • The process is rare, unstable, or cannot be measured
  • The first requirement is a fully autonomous agent
  • The project needs procurement, enterprise security review, or a multi-month rollout
